About UIL

UIL Uprety International Logistics was built on a simple belief — that the world’s most extraordinary animals deserve the world’s most reliable logistics.

 

Based in Cincinnati, Ohio, we specialize in the import, export, and domestic transportation of exotic wildlife for zoos, conservation organizations, private breeders, and research institutions. We combine the precision of Uprety Capital’s data-driven operational methodologies with deep regulatory expertise to deliver a logistics experience that’s safe, compliant, and built around your animals’ wellbeing. From CITES documentation to climate-controlled transport fleets, every system we’ve built exists to protect the animals in our care — and the institutions that trust us with them.
